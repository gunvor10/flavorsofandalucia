Trade questions

What travel advisors ask us

Do you offer net rates to travel advisors?

Yes. Every programme is quoted at net rates, confirmed in writing for the season you are selling. The rates are not commissionable — you add your own margin on top.

What group sizes do you handle?

From two guests upwards, with no upper limit — private programmes, events and celebrations alike. We have hosted family reunions and dinners for over 30 guests.

Which languages do your guides work in?

English and Norwegian with Gunvor, who also guides in Swedish and Danish. Through our licensed partner guides we cover French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish and Polish on request.

How fast do you reply to trade enquiries?

Within 24 hours, with the programme catalogue, net rates and availability.

Are you a licensed DMC?

Yes. We are a Seville-based DMC operated by Come to Spain Events SLU, a legally constituted Spanish travel agency, licence C.I.AN-41- 7397-2. See our travel guarantee.

Can you give trade references?

Yes. Ask us and we will put you in touch with travel advisors, agencies and operators we already work with.
