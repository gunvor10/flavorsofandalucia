Good to know

Questions about our foodie day tours

Which food destinations can I reach from Seville in a day?

Jerez and Cádiz for sherry, the Sierra de Aracena for Iberian ham, and an olive oil estate in the countryside outside Seville. Granada, Córdoba, Ronda and the white villages, Gibraltar and Doñana also work as day trips.

How long does a day tour take?

It differs by destination — each tour page carries its own duration in the fact strip at the top. The ham and sherry days are full days out of the city; the tastings closer to Seville take a few hours.

Are the day tours private?

Yes. The tours we run are private, your party only. Where an experience is run by a partner and shared with a small group, its page says so.

What do they cost?

Prices are on each tour page. Some depend on group size, and a few are quoted on request because the day is built around what you want to see.

Is transport included?

Each tour page lists exactly what is included. Days out of Seville are done with private transport unless the page says otherwise.

Can two destinations be combined?

Often, yes — or spread across two days, which usually makes for a better trip. Send us your dates and we will say what is realistic.
