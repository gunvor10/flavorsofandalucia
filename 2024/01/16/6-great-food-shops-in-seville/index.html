6 great food shops in Seville

Gunvor Guttormsen 16 January 2024 6 min read

Seville has a great food culture. Every morning, afternoon and evening the Sevillians find their way to one of the many café’s to share some tapas with friends or to grab a quick bite before work.

There are many dishes and products that are typical to Seville and the rest of Andalucia, and cherished by its inhabitants. Olive oil, Iberian ham, cheeses and olives are some examples of products the people here can’t get enough of. But where are the best places to get them? Today we’ll show you 6 great food shops in Seville that are excellent choices due to the love and passion they have for their products.

01

La Oleoteca de Sevilla

In the Arenal neighborhood, next to the Maestranza bullring and the Cathedral, is La Oleoteca de Sevilla, a place dedicated to ´the green gold´ where you can enjoy a wide catalog of the best olive oils in the world. In the Oleoteca you can try each of the products found.

You can do so without an appointment, they have space reserved in their store just for tastings. If you want to be sure you can take all the time you need, you can organize a private tasting at their store or any other location you could want. Experts will design a tasting for you and guide you to make sure you find the best possible olive oil for you.

02

Lama La Uva

The owner of Lama La Uva made a big career change to pursue her passion for the world of wine and experiences around gastronomy. The result is a stylish and modern wine store right behind the famous Las Setas.

All of the wines on their shelves are chosen with love and eye for quality. The team of Lama La Uva is very passionate about their work and love to explain the story behind each bottle you can find. They have a designated space where tastings can be arranged so you can try their wonderful wines and recommended pairings.

03

Flor de Sal

In 2010, Flor de Sal was born, a small wine store located on Carlos Cañal Street. They owe the success of their wine tastings to their extensive knowledge, genuine hospitality and the variety and exclusivity of their Spanish wines. All the wines they work with are exclusive to Flor de Sal.

In the tastings, people can learn from André Salla, the soul of Flor de Sal who has been in Spain since 2006. André has a long resume in hospitality from cooking and sommelier training in Brazil, to arriving in Spain and having all the training as a sommelier and approved trainer in Sherry wines as well as in multiple other wine types. To continue improving and offer more and better services, Flor de Sal has moved to Calle Espinosa y Cárcel, 10 in Nervión, where André has begun to offer wines by the glass.

04

Casa Orzaez

Casa Orzáez is a meeting point between small honest producers, conscious citizens and good food. The adventure began in 2003 with the creation of their dairy workshop, a cheese factory located in Castilblanco de los Arroyos, in the Sierra Norte of Seville. There they make cheeses from raw goat milk from the native Florida Sevillana breed, raised in extensive pasture livestock, through a manual process and using only their own natural ferments. In 2016 they decided to open their own store in their hometown, Seville.

Casa Orzáez is a multipurpose family project, in which you can sit at the table to try the products they offer, take them home and transform them to your liking, or both! The intention is to serve as a link between people concerned about what they consume and how that product affects or enriches our biodiversity and ourselves. They live by the philosophy “you are what you eat”.

05

Productos de la Sierra

Productos de la Sierra was started by a couple that in 2014 wanted to set up a small shop to bring the products of their village (Aracena) to Sevilla. They have 6 important ‘rules’ that lay the foundation for their business: 1. Products only from Andalusia or Extremadura. 2. Work with small farmers and cooperatives. 3. Taste all products once before selling them. 4. Meet with all producers at least four times a year. 5. Respect the environment. 6. Take care of customers like friends. Productos de la Sierra has a nice shop in the Casco Antiguo and an extensive online webshop where you can buy all kinds of conscious food products.

06

Jamoneria Jose Luis Romero

Jamoneria Jose Luis Romero has two locations in Seville. One at Mercado de la Encarnación (underneath Las Setas) and one at the iconic Mercado de Triana, which for a long time has been a popular choice to find local products. The brand specializes in Iberian ham but also sells over 60 kinds of national and international cheeses, cured meats, wine and gourmet products. Their tasting area mixes the traditional world of ham with the fresh and innovative trend of gourmet food markets.
