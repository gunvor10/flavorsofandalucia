Questions about our licences and guarantees

Is Flavors of Andalucia a licensed travel agency?

Yes. The website is owned by Come to Spain Events SLU, a legally constituted Spanish travel agency with headquarters in Seville. Our company licence is C.I.AN-41- 7397-2.

What is your VAT number?

Our international VAT number is ESB 90380494.

Am I financially protected if something goes wrong?

We satisfy all requirements for guarantees and insurances in case of bankruptcy, and for responsibilities in case of possible accidents or other damages connected with any tour we organize. Our insurance meets all Spanish Travel Guarantee schemes that we place with the Junta de Andalucía.

Are you an authorised DMC?

Yes. We hold an establishment licence showing that we meet all local requirements set by the authorities for an authorised DMC.

Do you hold any quality certification?

Yes. We have achieved the quality certificate and are included in the SICTED programme for tourist companies committed to Tourism Quality.
