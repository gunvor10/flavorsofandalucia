The experience

Tasting Market visit with tapas Lunch

The Best way to spend a wonderful Seville morning ! - Food market tasting tour in Seville with Gunvor !

The morning will be full of Flavors from a typical Spanish breakfast to a stroll at the local Triana market with ham and wine tasting.

We vill taste the best olive oil and have some tapas in one of the oldest tapas tavernas in Seville.

On this tour Gunvor will emerge you into the daily life in Seville through the food and wine, and she will dive you into the local history of the Arenal district.

Food in Seville, means so much more are not just eating; it is their way of life. But there’s so much more to our gastronomy than meets the eye. with a professional local guide by your side, you’ll uncover fascinating stories and traditions behind each dish, dispelling common myths and revealing the true essence of Andalusian food culture.

Food and drink are the keys to understanding the character of a place and its people. Prepare to be captivated by Andalusia’s culinary heritage—join Gunvor for an unforgettable food experience!
